# Filtre kaynakları

- Federal Reserve, Nominal Broad U.S. Dollar Index (DTWEXBGS): https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DTWEXBGS
- Alternative.me, Crypto Fear & Greed Index API: https://alternative.me/crypto/fear-and-greed-index/
- Fidelity, Average Directional Index (ADX): https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/adx
- Fidelity, Bollinger Bands: https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/technical-indicator-guide/bollinger-bands
- Binance Public Data: https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/

FRED serisi doğrudan ICE DXY değildir. Ücretsiz ve tekrarlanabilir dolar gücü
ölçümü olarak Federal Reserve'in geniş ticaret ağırlıklı dolar endeksi kullanılır.
